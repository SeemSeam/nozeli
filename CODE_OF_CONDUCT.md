# Community conduct

We want Nozeli's issues, reviews and discussions to be useful, welcoming and technically honest.

- Critique designs and evidence, not people. Respect different experience levels, languages and accessibility needs.
- No harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing or publication of private material.
- Ask before sharing another person's documents, screenshots, logs or identity. Redact credentials and user content.
- State what was observed and what remains uncertain. Do not misrepresent tests, platform support, ownership or license status.
- Keep discussions relevant and accept reasonable moderation decisions. Disagreement is welcome; repeated personal attacks or disruption are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, limit participation or block accounts. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and may be reconsidered when new information is provided.

Report an immediate policy concern with GitHub's **Report content** action; for non-sensitive project moderation requests open a minimal Issue without repeating private or abusive content. Sensitive security reports use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers must not require a reporter to expose private material publicly. This applies to project-hosted collaboration spaces and official project representation.
